Frequently Ask Question (FAQ)

Will whitening damage my teeth?

+

No, it is safe and does not harm enamel when done professionally.

Are veneers permanent?

+

They are long lasting but not permanent. They usually last for 10 years & with good care for 15 years or more. Should not bite anything hard with the teeth having veneers.

How long do composite build-ups last?

+

They usually last 5–7 years, depending on habits and care.

What is a digital smile makeover?

+

It uses digital tools to preview and plan your future smile before treatment begins.

Is the filling procedure painful?

+

Usually no. Local anesthesia may be used if the cavity is deep.

Is RCT painful?

+

Modern RCT is done under anesthesia and is mostly painless.

Will the extraction hurt?

+

No, it’s done under anesthesia. You may feel mild pressure, not pain.

Is scaling harmful for teeth?

+

No. It only removes plaque and tartar; enamel remains safe.

How long do traditional implants take to heal?

+

Typically around 3 months, but depending on bone quality and healing can be a bit longer too.

What is the main advantage of bi-cortical implants?

+

They provide strong stability even in patients with less bone volume.
